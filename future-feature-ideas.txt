Top 10 Future Feature Ideas
==========================

1. Side-by-side candidate comparison
   Compare shortlisted candidates against the same job criteria, with scores and resume evidence.

2. Reusable screening templates
   Save job criteria and settings to start similar screenings faster.

3. Scorecard exports
   Export candidate rankings, evidence, and recruiter notes to CSV or PDF.

4. Team review and assignments
   Assign candidates to reviewers and collect independent assessments.

5. Candidate pipeline
   Track candidates through stages such as New, In Review, Interview, and Decision.

6. Bulk resume upload
   Upload many resumes at once, with per-file parsing status and retry options.

7. Duplicate candidate detection
   Flag likely duplicate resumes or candidate records.

8. Audit history
   Record who changed criteria, reviewed scores, or deleted screening data.

9. Interview question suggestions
   Draft job-related questions grounded in a candidate's resume and the role criteria.

10. Data retention controls
    Configure when resumes and screening records are archived or deleted.
